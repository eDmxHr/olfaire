# Go-to-Market

Zero budget. No existing audience. Organic only.

---

## The Three Assets

1. **The product itself** — the layering chemistry feature is genuinely novel. No app does this properly.
2. **Fragrance community trust** — r/fragrance and Fragrantica are where the users live. Trust must be earned before anything is announced.
3. **The shareable signature card** — every shared card is an unpaid ad. Users create the content.

---

## Channel Priority

### Priority 1

**r/fragrance** (600K+ members)
Do not post "I made an app" cold. Spend 4–6 weeks contributing genuinely — answer layering questions, post chemistry breakdowns. Build karma and credibility first. When you have 50+ karma from real contributions, the launch post lands differently.

Post title: *"Built an app that analyses layering chemistry from your collection — beta testers wanted"*

**Fragrantica forums** (2.4M+ registered users)
Older, more knowledgeable demographic. A well-written post about the layering chemistry problem — framed as an observation, not a product pitch — can drive steady organic installs for years. Post in their Apps & Tools section.

Angle: *"Why most layering advice fails — and what the chemistry actually says"*

### Priority 2

**TikTok #PerfumeTok** — Don't make the videos. Your beta users do. The shareable signature card is designed for this. Seed it by asking beta users to share their first signature.

**YouTube fragrance creators (50K–200K subscribers)** — Reach out to mid-tier creators with early access. Short, specific, genuine email. Offer lifetime premium. Their honest review is worth more than any ad.

**Instagram @olfaire** — Create the account 6–8 weeks before launch. Post brand identity, Lumi, UI screenshots. No "coming soon." Just content that signals something refined is being built.

### Avoid at launch

**Product Hunt** — Works only with an existing audience to drive upvotes on day one. Without that, you'll land on page 3 with 12 upvotes. Save it for when you have 300+ users.

## Pre-Launch Sequence

### 6–8 weeks before launch
- Create @olfaire Instagram. Post brand identity weekly.
- Start contributing to r/fragrance. No Olfaire mentions yet.
- Draft the Fragrantica chemistry article. Don't post yet.
- Identify 15 YouTube creators. Prepare outreach. Don't send yet.

### 2 weeks before launch
- Post beta invitation on r/fragrance. Target 50–100 TestFlight slots.
- Publish Fragrantica chemistry article.
- Send creator outreach emails. Offer lifetime premium.
- Ask beta users to share their first signature card.

### Launch week
- Launch post on r/fragrance. Short, honest, no hype.
- Activate early adopter pricing: $2.99/month for first 500.
- Post Lumi in action on Instagram.
- Email creators who got early access: "Olfaire is live today."

### Post-launch (month 1)
- Check day-7 and day-30 retention. Is it above 40%? Below 25% means the core loop needs fixing.
- Read every review. Respond to every support message personally.
- Post one update per week on Instagram.
- Do not launch on Product Hunt yet.

### Month 3 (if retention is healthy)
- Product Hunt launch with existing user base.
- Press pitch to niche publications.
- Begin social features — needs real users to work.

## The Signature Card Growth Engine

Every shared signature card tells the viewer: *"I use an app that understands fragrance chemistry."*

**What makes a card shareable:**
- User-chosen name ("The Dark Contrast" not "Encre Noire + Chloé")
- Lumi's quote is literary — worth showing people
- Compatibility score gives a concrete data point to talk about
- Lumi watermark is subtle — present but not a banner ad
- Dark background looks good on any feed

**What to never do:**
- No "Download Olfaire" CTA on the card — kills shareability
- No App Store link embedded
- Don't gate card sharing behind premium — free users must be able to share
- Don't over-brand

## Realistic Year 1 Targets

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Beta users (2 weeks pre-launch) | 50 |
| Month 1 installs | 200 |
| Month 3 installs (if 1–2 creators post) | 1,000 |
| Paid users by Month 3 | ~60 (6% of 1K at $2.99) |

**The one rule:** Retention before growth. A hundred retained users are worth more than a thousand who bounced. Fix the core loop before scaling.

Day 7 retention target: 40%+. Below 25% means something is wrong — fix it before any marketing.
